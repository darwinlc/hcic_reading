How to use this map

Do not read this as a simple citation ranking. Citation counts favor older papers, while many of the most relevant 2024–2026 papers are still young. The ranking therefore blends influence, venue quality, mathematical depth, practical quant relevance, and usefulness as a gateway into a research thread. Citation figures marked * are approximate bands and should be refreshed from Google Scholar/Semantic Scholar before formal bibliometric use.

Core principle: for systematic trading, study the full chain data → representation → forecast distribution → uncertainty → portfolio optimization → execution → feedback. A high-accuracy predictor is not automatically a good strategy.

Important research hygiene

For financial ML, insist on walk-forward evaluation, purging/embargo where appropriate, realistic transaction costs, turnover constraints, point-in-time data, survivorship-bias controls, corporate-action handling, and statistical tests that account for repeated research. Treat Sharpe-ratio improvements in papers as hypotheses to reproduce, not facts to deploy.
